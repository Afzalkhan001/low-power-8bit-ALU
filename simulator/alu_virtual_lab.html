How the math was done

Circuit view

amber = 1 · slate = 0

The decoder turns the opcode into one enable per block. With operand isolation on, AND gates hold the inputs of every idle block at 0, so those blocks do not switch and burn no dynamic power. The 4:1 transmission-gate mux then passes the active block's result to Y.

Signal propagation

unit-delay simulation

When inputs change, each cell takes one delay to respond. Drag the slider or press play to watch the carry ripple and the outputs settle. Wrong values that flash on the way are glitches, and each glitch costs energy.
